## Executive Summary

We analyze the impact of wholesale supply on household spending and how seasonal changes affect final prices.

## Methodology

- Integration of CENADA (supply) and INEC (prices).
- Standardization of prices to cost per kilo.
- Cleaning of outliers, dates, and nomenclatures.
