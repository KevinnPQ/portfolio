## Executive Summary

An exploratory study on income distribution across essential goods. We observe how families in different quintiles prioritize their spending on food.

## Key Findings

- The lowest quintile allocates up to 40% of its income to the basic basket.
- Meat products represent the highest spending percentage in the upper quintiles.
- Bread and grains are a consistent nutritional staple across all strata.
